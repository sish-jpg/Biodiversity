Indian Palm Squirrel / Three-Striped Palm Squirrel
   Scientific Name: Funambulus palmarum
   Category: Mammal (Rodent)
   Location Observed: Pathway opposite AB2
   Habitat: Urban parks, gardens, scrublands, plantations and deciduous forests
