Indian Rat Snake / Dhaman / Oriental Rat Snake
Scientific Name: Ptyas mucosa
Category: Fauna (Snake)
Location Observed: L-Hostel
Habitat: Forests, grasslands, agricultural areas, wetlands, and human settlements
Ecological Importance: Helps control populations of rodents, frogs, lizards, and other small animals; plays an important role in maintaining ecosystem balance
Conservation Status: Least Concern (LC)
