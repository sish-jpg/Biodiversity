Mandevilla / Rocktrumpet / Chilean Jasmine
    Scientific Name: Mandevilla spp.
    Category: Plant (Evergreen Flowering Climber/Vine)
    Location Observed: Near Main Canteen
    Habitat: Warm, humid tropical and subtropical areas; cultivated on walls, fences and trellises
    Ecological Importance: Supports pollinators; provides vegetation cover and shelter
    Conservation Status: Not Evaluated for genus; varies by species
