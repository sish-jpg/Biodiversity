Drumstick Tree / Moringa Tree / Horseradish Tree
Scientific Name:  Moringa oleifera
Category: Plant (Small Tree)
Location Observed: L-Hostel
Habitat: Tropical and subtropical regions; commonly found in dry, open areas and cultivated landscapes
